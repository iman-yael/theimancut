## Series vs. history: watch-outs for narration
- Any claim that Bird and Cash listened to the final together at home is the **series' staging**. I found no source confirming it historically, so the script must say "the show stages…" and not "that night, Bird…".
- The portrayal of Cash as abusive comes from the series and from reviews. Documented history exists but is sensitive, so **say nothing about it in the pilot unless you approve a sourced line**.
