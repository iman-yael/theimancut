### CHR-001 · Cassius Clay / Muhammad Ali — Jaalen Best

- **Counterpart:** Real person. **Relationship:** Self.
- **Role in series (S1):** The lead. The series follows a young Louisville fighter from about 1960 to the 1964 heavyweight title fight. **(S2, also public history)** The finale centers on the Liston fight (per The Review Geek and TVMaze summaries).
- **ID references / visual continuity / voice notes / appearances / stats / standout moments:** PENDING-FOOTAGE.
- **Key relationships:** CHR-002 father · CHR-003 mother · CHR-004 brother · CHR-005 opponent · CHR-007 trainer · CHR-008 mentor/friend · CHR-009 cornerman · CHR-010 backer · CHR-011 religious leader.
- **Actor notes (separate):** Critics describe Best as a newcomer, and reviews widely praise the performance (Variety: "makes a star out of newcomer Jaalen Best"). Before any spotlight, verify his credits list from an authoritative source.
- **Historical background (H-UNCHECKED):** Born January 17, 1942, in Louisville, KY. Won light-heavyweight gold at the 1960 Rome Olympics. Beat Sonny Liston on February 25, 1964, in Miami Beach when Liston did not come out for the 7th round. Became publicly associated with the Nation of Islam and the name Muhammad Ali in early 1964. Died June 3, 2016.
- **Sources to check:** Jonathan Eig, *Ali: A Life* (2017); Thomas Hauser, *Muhammad Ali: His Life and Times* (1991); Muhammad Ali Center (Louisville); contemporaneous newspaper archives.
- **Sensitive material:** Racism (Jim Crow Louisville), religion/NOI, the family violence storyline, possible slurs in period dialogue.

### CHR-002 · Cassius "Cash" Clay Sr. — Omari Hardwick

- **Counterpart:** Real person. **Relationship:** Father.
- **Role in series (S1):** Head of the Clay household. Reviews describe a man who drinks heavily, cheats on Bird, is violent at home, and loves his family. Reviewers also note his conflict over watching his son reach heights he once wanted for himself.
- **Footage fields:** PENDING-FOOTAGE.
- **Actor notes (separate):** Hardwick is widely known for *Power*. Reviews praise his performance in this role (Revolt, Wherever I Look).
- **Historical background (H-UNCHECKED):** A sign painter and muralist in Louisville, named for the 19th-century Kentucky abolitionist Cassius Marcellus Clay.
- **Domestic violence (H-UNCHECKED, HIGH CARE):** Secondary sources (Wikipedia mirrors) say Odessa took him to court more than once for physical abuse, and that he was arrested for assault and battery and other offences, typically after drinking. **Do not publish this until it has been checked against Eig's *Ali: A Life* and, ideally, Louisville court records.** I could not reach a primary source.
- **Show vs history:** Any specific on-screen incident should be labeled as dramatized unless matched to a documented record.
- **Content opportunities:** An actor spotlight on Hardwick's range. Hold any DV-related clip until it has been reviewed under the sensitivity rules.
- **Sensitive material:** Domestic violence, alcoholism, infidelity. Never frame abuse scenes as entertainment or use them as the hook.

### CHR-003 · Odessa "Bird" Clay — Dana Gourrier

- **Counterpart:** Real person. **Relationship:** Mother.
- **Role in series (S1):** The emotional anchor of the household. Reviews call her "an absolute force," say she gives the series "much of its warmth," and note that she gives Bird's anger a clear reason. One critique: she gets too little time away from Cash and the boys.
- **Footage fields:** PENDING-FOOTAGE.
- **Actor notes (separate):** Gourrier's prior credits need to be verified before use.
- **Historical background (H-UNCHECKED):** Born Odessa Lee Grady in Kentucky; lived 1917–1994. She worked as a domestic worker, and Ali often credited her influence. The family nickname "Bird" is attested in the series credits; check the historical attestation in Eig.
- **Content opportunities:** **Top candidate** for the first actor spotlight and the presenter test (see 04).
- **Sensitive material:** She is depicted as a DV survivor. Center her agency rather than the abuse.

### CHR-004 · Rudy Clay (Rahman / Rahaman Ali) — Kai Parham

- **Counterpart:** Real person. **Relationship:** Younger brother.
- **Role (S1):** Cassius's younger brother and one of his closest confidants (per reviews).
- **Footage fields:** PENDING-FOOTAGE.
- **Historical background (H-UNCHECKED):** Born 1943. Also boxed professionally. Joined the Nation of Islam and took the name Rahman Ali. **Spelling conflict:** sources use both "Rahman" and "Rahaman", so confirm a preferred form. Check his current living status before writing in present or past tense.
- **Content opportunities:** A "Brothers" relationship mashup (CHR-001 + CHR-004), which carries low sensitivity risk.

### CHR-005 · Sonny Liston — Amin Joseph

- **Counterpart:** Real person. **Relationship:** Opponent; the champion Clay beat in 1964.
- **Role (S1):** Heavyweight champion and Ali's rival. **(S2/S3)** The finale fight.
- **Footage fields:** PENDING-FOOTAGE.
- **Actor notes (separate):** Joseph is known for *Snowfall* (Jerome Saint).
- **Historical background (H-UNCHECKED):** Born Charles L. Liston in Arkansas; **his birth year is genuinely disputed** (records vary, roughly 1930–1932). Served prison time in Missouri for robbery. Won the title from Floyd Patterson in 1962. Lost to Clay in February 1964 and again in the 1965 rematch. Found dead in Las Vegas in January 1971, and the circumstances are debated.
- **Show vs history:** Organized-crime ties and the "did he quit?" questions about both fights are **contested allegations**. Present them as disputed, never as fact.
- **Sensitive material:** Incarceration, alleged mob ties, the stereotyped "menace" framing in period press. Avoid repeating it uncritically.

### CHR-007 · Angelo Dundee — Giovanni Ribisi

- **Counterpart:** Real person. **Relationship:** Trainer.
- **Historical background (H-UNCHECKED):** Born Angelo Mirena, 1921–2012. Trained out of the 5th Street Gym in Miami Beach. Became Clay's trainer around 1960 and stayed in his corner for most of his career. A widely reported story from the 1964 fight: Clay's eyes burned between rounds, and Dundee pushed him back out. **Verify the details and whether the series depicts it.**
- **Footage fields:** PENDING-FOOTAGE.

### CHR-008 · Malcolm X — Michael Ealy

- **Counterpart:** Real person. **Relationship:** Mentor and friend; later estranged.
- **Historical background (H-UNCHECKED):** Lived 1925–1965. Grew close to Clay around 1962–63 and was in Miami before the Liston fight. Broke with the NOI in March 1964, after which Ali sided with Elijah Muhammad. Assassinated on February 21, 1965. Ali later expressed regret over the break; check the exact quotation in Ali's memoir *The Soul of a Butterfly* (2004) and only quote verbatim.
- **Sensitive material:** Religion, racism, political violence, the assassination (outside the series window).

### CHR-009 · Drew "Bundini" Brown — Reno Wilson

- **Historical background (H-UNCHECKED):** Lived 1928–1987. Assistant trainer and motivator in Ali's corner. **Attribution caution:** "Float like a butterfly, sting like a bee" is often credited to Bundini, but accounts vary. Treat authorship as uncertain.

### CHR-010 · William Faversham Jr. — Aleks Paunovic

- **Role:** Described as a former financial backer from an influential theater family (eldest son of Broadway actor William Faversham Sr.).
- **Historical background (H-UNCHECKED):** A key organizer of the Louisville Sponsoring Group, the group of white Louisville businessmen who financed Clay's early professional career starting in 1960.
- **Content opportunity:** The question "who bankrolled Cassius Clay?" makes a strong low-sensitivity deep dive.
- **Actor notes (separate):** Paunovic is Canadian, and an interview describes him as a former boxer (single source, so verify).

### CHR-011 · Elijah Muhammad — Hill Harper · CHR-012 · Herbert Muhammad — Jerod Haynes

- **Credit status:** V2 (weaker). Confirm against on-screen end credits before naming either actor in a post.
- **Historical background (H-UNCHECKED):** Elijah Muhammad (1897–1975) led the Nation of Islam and publicly bestowed the name "Muhammad Ali" in March 1964. Herbert Muhammad (1929–2008), his son, became Ali's manager from about 1966, which is after the series window, so his on-screen role may be limited.
- **Sensitive material:** Religion. Present NOI history neutrally and with sources.

## 4. Counting rules (apply when footage arrives)

- **Shot:** One continuous camera take, from cut to cut. A dissolve counts as a boundary.
- **Scene:** A continuous narrative unit in a single location and continuous time. A new scene starts when the location changes **or** when time jumps (a cut that implies elapsed time). A montage is logged as one scene tagged `MONTAGE`. In cross-cutting, each location strand is a separate scene, linked by a `SEQ-` ID. Flashbacks and dreams are separate scenes tagged `FLASHBACK`.
- **Per character:** Count a scene once even if the character appears in many shots. Record visible scenes, voice-only scenes (off-screen/V.O./phone), and mentions-only in separate columns. Screen time counts only visible frames. Overlapping characters each get the full overlap.
- **Timestamps:** Log both `file_tc` (from 00:00:00 of the delivered file) and `episode_tc` (from the episode's first frame of program, after any studio logo). Record the offset per file.
- All counts stay labeled **PROVISIONAL** until every episode the character could appear in has been reviewed.
