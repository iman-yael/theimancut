# Editorial, sensitivity, moderation and corrections policy

## 1. Three layers, always separate
1. **What the series depicts:** "In the series, this scene depicts…"
2. **Iman's interpretation:** "The scene suggests…" (proposed to Iman for approval)
3. **Documented history:** "Historical sources document…", only with a `supported` claim (CLM) and sources.

Never: a scripted scene as evidence of real misconduct; diagnoses; invented quotes; the actor blamed for the character's actions; new dialogue or lip movement generated for any performer.

## 2. Sensitive-content review (per clip)
Flags: domestic violence · sexual assault · coercive control · racism / slurs · child abuse · suicide/self-harm · graphic injury. Record each as **depicted / implied / historically documented** in the scene record (`scene.sensitive_json`) and on the character sheet.

Per-clip review row:
`Content flags | Context preserved | Content note | Resource decision | Historical verification | Platform concerns | Ready / Needs review`

For every sensitive clip, offer three options: (1) a contextual, non-graphic edit; (2) an analysis-led edit with minimal excerpts; (3) **skip** when responsible context can't be preserved.

Rules: no looped assaults; no graphic injury in thumbnails; no romanticised intimidation; no victim-blaming hooks or questions; slurs never repeated unnecessarily and never used in tags; extra care with minors; never invite viewers to disclose trauma publicly. Editing never evades moderation or copyright detection.

Content-note wording (before the material, and in the caption): "Content note: domestic violence." · "Content note: racist language." · "Content note: sexual assault references."

## 3. Support resources
**None verified yet.** For every resource, verify the official website, country served, current contact methods, hours and the organisation's safety/device-privacy guidance on the official site at the time of publication. Until then, use:

> `[RESOURCE: specialist DV support org — COUNTRY — VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION]`

Never reuse a number from memory or a search snippet. Label the country. A US line does not serve international viewers; prefer a link to a profile resource page (captions aren't reliably clickable). For suicide/self-harm, use an approved local crisis-resource lookup. The account offers no counselling, crisis response or monitored messages, and must never imply that it does.

## 4. Comment moderation plan (posts touching abuse or racism)
- Hold for review: slurs, threats, doxxing, harassment of performers or real families, victim-blaming, graphic descriptions.
- Do not remove respectful disagreement or criticism.
- Optional pinned comment: "*[Actor] plays [Character] in The Greatest (Prime Video). This scene is a dramatization; historical notes and sources are in the description. [Verified resource line, if relevant.]*"
- Reply to personal disclosures (draft; send only with Iman's approval): "*Thank you for trusting us with this. We're not able to offer support here, but [verified resource for your country] can help. Take care of yourself.*" Never ask for details.
- Nothing is posted, replied to or messaged without Iman's authorization.

## 5. Scene integrity
Watch the surrounding scene before cutting. Preserve who initiated the exchange and what each reaction responds to. Never join separate lines or reactions to imply an event. Label non-chronological compilations ("Thematic order, not chronological"). Commentary is visually distinct from original dialogue.

## 6. Corrections and takedowns
1. **Pause:** any scheduled post linked to an affected export (via `dependency` + `stale_flag`) is listed for Iman to pause.
2. **Correct:** fix the record in the catalog (new revision), regenerate docs, prepare corrected caption/export as a new version.
3. **Replace:** the caption is edited only with approval; the old wording is kept in `CORRECTIONS_LOG.md`.
4. **Withdraw:** if an edit misrepresents a scene, recommend withdrawal and log it.
5. **Copyright notices / platform enforcement:** flag to Iman. No automatic disputes and no reposting of removed material.

## 7. Disclosures
AI narration or presenter: on-screen "AI presenter based on my likeness" or "Narration: AI voice based on Iman's voice", plus the platform AI label. Sponsorship or affiliation: disclose only if documented; otherwise "Not affiliated with Prime Video or the production."
